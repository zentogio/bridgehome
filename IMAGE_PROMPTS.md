# Prompt สร้างรูป Bridge Home × สายใย

วิธีใช้: คัดลอก prompt แล้วสร้างทีละรูป (หรือทั้งชุดในแชตเดียวเพื่อให้สไตล์เหมือนกัน)
- รูปทั่วไป → บันทึกเป็น `.jpg` ที่ `public/images/<ชื่อ>.jpg` (เว็บจะแทนที่ช่องว่างให้อัตโนมัติ)
- โลโก้ → บันทึกเป็น `public/brand/logo.png` (พื้นหลังโปร่งใส 1024×1024)

---

## A) Prompt โลโก้ (PNG)

Minimal app icon logo for "Bridge Home × สายใย", an elderly-care family app in Thailand.
Concept: a simple house outline (peaked roof, rounded corners) in white, with one thin warm-gold silk thread
that arcs beneath it like a bridge, ending in two small round knots on both sides, as if the thread connects two homes.
Flat vector, geometric, confident, friendly, no gradients, no shadows, no text, no mockup.
Colors: deep indigo-dye blue #2B3A8F background rounded square (corner radius ~22%), white house, golden amber thread #F2A93B.
Centered, generous padding, readable at 32px. 1:1, 1024x1024. Provide two versions: indigo background, and transparent background with indigo house.

---

## B) Prompt ชุดรูปประกอบแอป (ใช้ prompt เดียว สร้าง 5 รูปสไตล์เดียวกัน)

Create a consistent set of 5 editorial flat illustrations for a Thai elderly-care family app.
SHARED STYLE: warm hand-drawn flat vector with soft grain texture, slightly rounded shapes, calm and dignified (never childish or cartoonish),
Thai setting and people (Thai faces, Thai homes, tropical plants), limited palette: deep indigo #2B3A8F, white, soft lavender-grey #EEF0F8,
warm amber #F2A93B used ONLY for a thin silk thread that appears in every picture connecting people or objects. No text, no logos, no UI mockups.
Natural morning light. Generous empty space around subjects.

1. hero.jpg (4:3, 1600x1200): split composition, two homes. Left: a Thai woman in her 40s in a Bangkok condo looking at her phone with a relieved smile.
   Right: her elderly mother (79) in a wooden Ayutthaya house, sitting by a window, talking gently to a small glowing smartphone.
   A single thin amber thread loops from the phone on the left across the empty middle to the phone on the right.
2. nongyai.jpg (1:1, 800x800): friendly avatar of "น้องใย", an AI companion shown as a warm, abstract soft-round figure made of looped amber thread
   forming a gentle smiling face and a tiny heart, on indigo background. Not a robot, not a human, comforting.
3. discharge.jpg (4:3): a daughter's hands photographing a hospital discharge paper and medicine bag on a wooden table with her phone;
   amber thread rises from the paper into a neat checklist.
4. nurse.jpg (1:1, 800x800): portrait of a kind Thai community nurse in a clean uniform with a tablet, calm expression, indigo background with a tiny amber thread near the lapel.
5. family-team.jpg (4:3): three siblings of different ages in a video call from different places (office, kitchen, car) all holding phones,
   connected by one amber thread to a bedridden-care room scene in the center with an elderly man and caregiver turning him gently.

Keep the same artist style, same palette and the same thread motif in all five.

## C) Prompt มาสคอต "น้องใย" (character design)

Design an original mascot character named "Nong Yai" (น้องใย), the friendly AI companion of a Thai elderly-care family app.
CONCEPT: a small, soft, round silkworm-in-cocoon creature (Thai silk heritage; "yai" = silk thread). Body is a plump egg-shaped cocoon of warm silk-white
with a gentle shade at the bottom, wrapped by a thin golden-amber silk thread that loops around the belly. One small curl of golden thread sprouts from the top of the head like a cowlick.
FACE: two big glossy dark-indigo eyes with a tiny white highlight, rosy-peach cheeks, a small warm smile. Calm, trustworthy, grandchild-like. Not a robot, not a bug, no antennae, no wings.
LIMBS: tiny rounded stubby arms and two little oval feet in the same silk-white. Height about 2 heads tall, chubby, easy to read at 48px.
STYLE: clean flat vector mascot, thick uniform deep-indigo outline (#2B3A8F-ish), soft cel shading, minimal details, friendly proportions, suitable for elderly users (high contrast, simple shapes), cute but dignified, never childish.
PALETTE: silk-white #FBF7EC, outline/eyes deep indigo #2B3A8F, thread amber #F2A93B, cheeks peach #F4B8A0.
OUTPUT: character sheet on plain white background, no text: (1) front view hero pose smiling, (2) listening pose with one hand cupped at the ear and head tilted,
(3) speaking pose with open mouth and small golden sound-wave arcs, (4) thinking pose looking up with three small thread-dots, (5) worried/caring pose with soft raised brows,
(6) sleepy pose with closed eyes, (7) waving hello, (8) holding the end of a long golden thread that trails off to the right toward a small smartphone.
Then export the hero pose alone as a square 1024x1024 PNG with transparent background -> save as public/brand/nongyai.png

## D) Prompt น้องใยแบบเด็ก (แทนข้อ C — ใช้ข้อนี้)

Design an original character "Nong Yai" (น้องใย), a sweet Thai child (about 7-8 years old, gender-neutral or a girl with a short bob) who acts as the cheerful "grandchild" companion in a Thai elderly-care family app.
Feels like the grandchild every grandparent wishes would call every morning: gentle, polite, attentive, never loud.
LOOK: round friendly face, big warm dark-brown eyes with a small highlight, rosy cheeks, soft smile. Short dark hair with ONE small golden silk-thread curl sticking up like a cowlick.
Outfit: simple soft indigo (#2B3A8F) cotton shirt with a small white collar, light trousers or skirt, a thin golden-amber silk thread worn like a friendship bracelet / loosely looped around the wrist and trailing behind.
Proportions: chibi-friendly, about 3 heads tall, simple shapes that stay readable at 48px.
STYLE: match the existing app illustration style: soft painterly flat vector, gentle shading, clean outlines in deep indigo, warm natural light, calm and dignified, high contrast, no text, no logos.
PALETTE: deep indigo #2B3A8F, white, soft lavender-grey #EEF0F8, skin tones natural Thai, thread amber #F2A93B used only for the silk thread.
OUTPUT: one character sheet on plain white background, no text:
(1) front hero pose smiling with both hands clasped politely in a "wai" greeting
(2) listening pose: hand cupped behind the ear, head tilted, soft golden sound arcs
(3) speaking pose: open friendly mouth, one hand gesturing, golden sound arcs
(4) thinking pose: finger on chin, looking up, three small thread-dots
(5) caring / worried pose: soft raised brows, hand on chest
(6) cheering pose: both arms up, small golden sparkles
(7) sleepy pose: yawning with rubbing eyes
(8) holding a long golden thread that trails to the right and ends at a small smartphone
Then export the hero pose alone as a square 1024x1024 PNG with transparent background -> public/brand/nongyai.png
